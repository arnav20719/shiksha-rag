Read this first. Only NIRF 2025 ranks (Ministry of Education, released 4 Sep 2025) and a few NAAC grades are official-grade data here. NIRF 2026 had not been published as of 24 Sep 2026. Fees,
placement packages and reviews come from commercial portals (Collegedunia, Shiksha, Careers360, Zollege) and disagree with each other, so they are shown as ranges. Fields I could not verify are
marked NA - verify. Nothing was estimated or invented.
1. Key findings
• Bihar has only two engineering institutions in the NIRF 2025 top 100: IIT Patna (Engineering #19, Overall #36) and NIT Patna (Engineering #53). No Bihar private engineering college
appears in the ranked list.
• Bihar's ranked strengths beyond engineering: AIIMS Patna (Medical #27), IIM Bodh Gaya (Management #31), Chanakya National Law University (Law #17), CUSB Gaya (Law #23),
Rajendra Prasad Central Agricultural University (Agriculture #14).
• Fee gap is huge. State government engineering colleges (MIT Muzaffarpur, GCE Gaya, Bakhtiyarpur, Chandi) charge roughly Rs 10K to Rs 1.4 lakh in total per portal listings; private
colleges run from about Rs 2.8 lakh to Rs 16.6 lakh. Placement averages for private colleges are mostly Rs 3.5-6.6 LPA.
• Medical: government MBBS in Bihar costs from about Rs 5.9K (AIIMS Patna, total) to about Rs 4.5 lakh (IGIMS, total); private colleges such as Netaji Subhas Medical College list totals up
to about Rs 89 lakh.
• BSCC (Bihar Student Credit Card): up to Rs 4 lakh, processed through DRCC verification. The interest rate is reported three different ways by secondary sources, so confirm on the
official portal before quoting it to students (section 6).
• Nationally: IITs hold the top 8 engineering ranks in NIRF 2025; NIT Tiruchirappalli (#9) is the only NIT in the top 10; BITS Pilani (#11) is the top private institution.

6. Bihar Student Credit Card (BSCC): what is confirmed and what conflicts
Broadly agreed across sources
• Loan up to Rs 4 lakh, no collateral. Launched 2 Oct 2016 under the 7 Nishchay programme; apply on 7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in (official portal), then complete DRCC (District
Registration and Counselling Centre) verification with admission proof.
• Applicant must be a Bihar resident who has passed Class 12; a confirmed admission letter is needed; a maximum age of 25 at application is reported by one source.
• Valid for recognised institutions across India, not only in Bihar. MeriShiksha publishes state-wise pages (Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Jharkhand); its Uttar Pradesh page states about 150
eligible colleges in UP.

• Recommended tagging in your database: Yes (verified) only when the college appears in the official DRCC/portal list; Likely for government institutions; Verify for every private college.
The tables above use exactly these three labels.
• The official guidelines page lists a circular dated 17.10.2025 (Letter No. 355), which is the first document to read for current rules.
